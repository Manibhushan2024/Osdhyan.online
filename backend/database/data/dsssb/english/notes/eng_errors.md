# Error Spotting, Sentence Improvement & Fill in the Blanks

**In 30 seconds**
- Three formats: (1) a sentence split into **four segments** — pick the one with the error (sometimes "No error"); (2) a bracketed/underlined part — pick the better version or **"No substitution required"**; (3) **fill in the blank** with a word, preposition or tense.
- In 2023–24 DSSSB asked: **inversion after *No sooner / Barely*** ⭐ (*No sooner had the company launched…*; *Barely had they won…*), **word choice** ⭐ (*limping* back to normalcy, an *extended* period, *scorching* heat), **wrong part of speech** ⭐ (*detection dog*, *her being honest*), and wrong prepositions and conjunctions dressed as error spotting ⭐.
- Read the whole sentence first, find the **subject and main verb**, then check the segment list in this order: **verb (tense/agreement) → preposition → noun/pronoun form → adjective/adverb → conjunction/word order → redundancy**.
- "No substitution required" is right about **1 time in 4–5** — choose it only after testing every option in the sentence.
- Most errors repeat: *despite of*, *prefer… than*, *didn't knew*, *one of the boys who has*, *advices*, *more taller*, *returned back*, *hardly… than*, *if he will come*, *between you and I*.
- The grammar rules behind every item are in the Grammar chapter; this chapter is the **checklist and the catalogue of repeated errors**.

## 1. What DSSSB asked (2023–24)

| Paper item | Format | Correct form / rule | ⭐ |
|---|---|---|---|
| *No sooner had the company launch its new product than…* | improvement | **No sooner had the company launched** — past perfect (had + V3) after inversion | ⭐ |
| *Barely has they won the match when…* | improvement | **Barely had they won** — past perfect, inverted | ⭐ |
| *Post covid, air travel is hopping back to normalcy* | improvement | **limping back** — slow, difficult recovery | ⭐ |
| *'permacrisis' … any widespread period of instability* | improvement | **extended period** | ⭐ |
| *after the flaming heat of the summer* | improvement | **scorching heat** | ⭐ |
| *The Mysore palace is gearing up for the winter festival* | improvement | **No substitution required** (*gear up for* = prepare) | ⭐ |
| *The ideal detect dog / is extremely energetic…* | segments | **detection dog** — a noun is needed as modifier, not a verb | ⭐ |
| *I appreciate her being honesty…* | segments | **her being honest** — adjective after *being* | ⭐ |
| *Whether I do not agree with her views, I respect her* | segments | **Though/Although** | ⭐ |
| *engage on dust bathing / walk through the beach / pour into the flask into the cups* | segments | **engage in / walk along / pour from** | ⭐ |

## 2. The 6-step method for error spotting

| Step | Ask yourself | Typical catch |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Subject → verb | Is the subject singular or plural? Does the verb agree? | *The furniture **are*** ✘ → *is* |
| 2. Tense | Do time words (*since, for, yesterday, by the time, if*) fit the tense? | *I am living here **since** 2010* ✘ → *have been living* |
| 3. Verb form | After *did/does/to/can* → base form; after *has/had/being/been* → V3; after *being* + adjective | *didn't **knew*** ✘ → *know* |
| 4. Preposition | Is the fixed preposition right? Is one missing or extra? | *discuss **about*** ✘; *absent **in*** ✘ → *from* |
| 5. Word form | Noun/adjective/adverb in the right slot? Article right? Pronoun case right? | *chewed **slow*** ✘; *between you and **I*** ✘ |
| 6. Linking & style | Pairs (*neither…nor, hardly…when*), word order, redundancy, double negatives, parallel structure | *returned **back*** ✘; *hardly **never*** ✘ |

**Tip**: the segment with the error usually contains the verb or a preposition. If all four segments look right, look again at the first one — DSSSB often puts the error at the start (*The ideal detect dog*, *Whether I do not agree*).

## 3. Catalogue of repeated errors

### 3.1 Nouns

| Rule | ✘ Wrong | ✔ Correct |
|---|---|---|
| Uncountable nouns have no plural and take singular verbs: advice, information, furniture, luggage, baggage, scenery, machinery, equipment, poetry, stationery, news | She gave me many *advices*. The *sceneries* of Kashmir *are*… | She gave me **much advice / a piece of advice**. The **scenery** of Kashmir **is**… |
| Some nouns are always plural: cattle, police, people, poultry, clergy | The police *is* coming. | The police **are** coming. |
| Some nouns look plural but are singular: news, mathematics, physics, economics, measles, innings | The news *are* good. | The news **is** good. |
| Number + noun used as an adjective stays singular | a *ten-rupees* note; a *five-years* plan | a **ten-rupee** note; a **five-year** plan |
| Dozen, hundred, thousand, score after a number stay singular | three *dozens* eggs | **three dozen** eggs |
| *Hair* (on the head) is uncountable | Her *hairs are* black. | Her **hair is** black. |
| Use a noun as a modifier, not a verb ⭐ | The ideal *detect* dog | The ideal **detection** dog |
| Cousin brother / cousin sister are not standard | my *cousin brother* | my **cousin** |

### 3.2 Pronouns

| Rule | ✘ Wrong | ✔ Correct |
|---|---|---|
| Object case after a preposition | between you and *I* | between you and **me** |
| Subject case after *than/as* (verb understood) | He is taller than *me*. | He is taller than **I** (am). |
| *One … one's* | One should love *his* country. | One should love **one's** country. |
| *Each other* (two), *one another* (more than two) | The two sisters love *one another*. | The two sisters love **each other**. |
| *Who* for subject, *whom* for object | The boy *whom* came first… | The boy **who** came first… |
| *Everyone/each/neither* + singular pronoun | Everyone must bring *their* own book (exam style ✘) | Everyone must bring **his or her** own book. |
| A reflexive pronoun cannot be the subject | *Myself* am a teacher. | **I** am a teacher. |
| Don't repeat a subject with a pronoun | The Principal *he* is coming. | The Principal is coming. |

### 3.3 Verbs and verb forms

| Rule | ✘ Wrong | ✔ Correct |
|---|---|---|
| *Did/does/do* + base verb | She didn't *knew*. | She didn't **know**. |
| Modal + base verb | He can *swims*. | He can **swim**. |
| Present perfect with *since/for* + continuing action | I *am knowing* him *since* 2010. | I **have known** him since 2010. |
| Definite past time → simple past | I *have finished* it yesterday. | I **finished** it yesterday. |
| Earlier of two past actions → past perfect | When I reached, the train *left*. | When I reached, the train **had left**. |
| *If* clause never takes *will* | If he *will come*, I shall tell him. | If he **comes**, I shall tell him. |
| *Would have* belongs in the main clause, not the *if* clause | If you *would have asked*, I would have helped. | If you **had asked**, I would have helped. |
| *Suggest / recommend / insist* + *that … (should) + V1* | He suggested *me to take* a taxi. | He suggested **that I take/should take** a taxi. |
| *Let, make, see, hear, watch, help* + object + base verb (no *to*) | He made me *to cry*. | He made me **cry**. |
| Gerund after a preposition | He is fond *of swim*. / I look forward *to meet* you. | fond **of swimming**; look forward **to meeting** you |
| *Being* + adjective, not noun ⭐ | her being *honesty* | her **being honest** |
| *Lay* (put; laid) vs *lie* (rest; lay, lain) | He *laid* on the bed. | He **lay** on the bed. |
| *Hang* (hanged = executed; hung = suspended) | The picture was *hanged*. | The picture was **hung**. |
| *Raise* (transitive) vs *rise* (intransitive) | Prices have *raised*. | Prices have **risen**. |

**Gerund or infinitive?**

| Followed by gerund (-ing) | Followed by infinitive (to + V1) | Both (change of meaning) |
|---|---|---|
| avoid, enjoy, finish, mind, miss, practise, consider, deny, admit, postpone, risk, suggest, keep, can't help, look forward to, object to, be used to, be accustomed to, it's no use, worth | want, decide, hope, agree, refuse, promise, plan, expect, afford, manage, offer, learn, wish, pretend, fail, threaten | *stop* (stop smoking = quit; stop to smoke = pause in order to smoke), *remember/forget* (remember locking = memory of past; remember to lock = duty), *try*, *regret* |
| ✔ I **enjoy reading**. ✘ I enjoy *to read*. | ✔ He **decided to leave**. ✘ He decided *leaving*. | ✔ He **stopped smoking** last year. |

### 3.4 Adjectives, adverbs and comparison

| Rule | ✘ Wrong | ✔ Correct |
|---|---|---|
| No double comparative | She is *more taller* than her sister. | She is **taller** than her sister. |
| *Very* + positive; *much* + comparative | *very much beautiful*; *very better* | **very** beautiful; **much** better |
| Comparison inside a group: *any other* | He is taller than *any boy* in his class. | He is taller than **any other boy** in his class. |
| Compare like with like | The climate of Shimla is better than *Delhi*. | …better than **that of** Delhi. |
| *Senior/junior/prior/superior/inferior/prefer* + *to* | He is senior *than* me. | He is senior **to** me. |
| *Fewer* (count) / *less* (mass) | *less* mistakes | **fewer** mistakes |
| Adverb after an action verb ⭐ | He chewed *slow*. | He chewed **slowly**. |
| Adjective after a linking verb | The food tastes *deliciously*. | The food tastes **delicious**. |
| *Elder* (family, not followed by *than*) / *older* | He is *elder than* me. | He is **older than** me. |
| *Enough* comes after an adjective | He is *enough strong*. | He is **strong enough**. |
| Order of adjectives: opinion–size–age–shape–colour–origin–material | a *red big old* car | a **big old red** car |

### 3.5 Conjunctions, pairs and word order

| Rule | ✘ Wrong | ✔ Correct |
|---|---|---|
| *Though/although* without *but* ⭐ | *Though* he was tired, *but* he worked. | **Though** he was tired, he worked. |
| *Whether* does not express contrast ⭐ | *Whether* I do not agree, I respect her. | **Though** I do not agree, I respect her. |
| *Neither … nor* (not *or*); *either … or* | Neither Ram *or* Shyam came. | Neither Ram **nor** Shyam came. |
| *No sooner … than*; *hardly/scarcely/barely … when* ⭐ | Hardly had he reached *than* the train left. | Hardly had he reached **when** the train left. |
| Inversion after a negative opening word ⭐ | No sooner *he had left* than… / Not only *he is* a singer… | **No sooner had he left** than… / **Not only is he** a singer… |
| *Unless/until* already negative | Unless you *don't* work hard… | **Unless you work** hard… |
| *Not only … but also* parallel | He *not only* plays the guitar *but also* the piano. (✘ uneven) | He plays **not only** the guitar **but also** the piano. |
| *Such … as / such … that*; *so … that*; *as … as* | He is *so* weak *as* he cannot walk. | He is **so** weak **that** he cannot walk. |
| *Reason … is that* (not *because*) | The reason is *because* he was ill. | The reason is **that** he was ill. |
| Indirect question keeps statement order | I asked him where *was he* going. | I asked him **where he was** going. |
| Double negative | She *hardly never* visits. / I *don't know nothing*. | She **hardly ever** visits. / I **know nothing**. |

**Inversion openers** ⭐ (auxiliary before subject): *No sooner had…, Hardly had…, Scarcely had…, Barely had…, Never have I…, Seldom do we…, Rarely does he…, Not only is/does…, Only then did…, Little did he know…, Under no circumstances should you…, Should you need help… (= if), Had I known… (= if), Were I you… (= if)*.

### 3.6 Redundancy (superfluous words)

| ✘ Wrong | ✔ Correct |
|---|---|
| *returned back*, *revert back*, *repeat again* | returned, revert, repeat |
| *despite of* | despite / in spite of |
| *discuss about*, *enter into the room*, *reach at the station*, *order for* | discuss, enter the room, reach the station, order |
| *each and every one of them* (wordy) | each of them |
| *the reason is because* | the reason is that |
| *free gift*, *final outcome*, *past history*, *future plans*, *advance planning* | gift, outcome, history, plans, planning |
| *cousin brother*, *return back home* | cousin, return home |
| *more better*, *most best* | better, best |
| *both … as well as* | both … and |
| *ascend up*, *descend down*, *collaborate together* | ascend, descend, collaborate |

## 4. Sentence improvement

DSSSB uses sentence improvement for two things: **grammar** (tense, inversion, agreement) and **the precise word** (collocation and connotation) ⭐.

| Type | How to decide | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Grammar | Put each option in the sentence and run the 6-step check | *No sooner had the company **launched**…* ⭐ |
| Connotation / tone ⭐ | Ask whether the word's feeling fits the context (positive, negative, slow, fast) | *hopping back* (lively) ✘ → **limping back** (slow, struggling) for a weak recovery |
| Collocation ⭐ | Which word naturally goes with the noun? | **extended** period, **scorching** heat, **fast** train, **fully** aware, **heavy** rain |
| Precise word | Choose the word with the exact meaning | A doctor **prescribes** (✘ *writes/describes*) medicine |
| No substitution | Only after every option makes the sentence worse or no better | *The palace is **gearing up for** the festival* ⭐ — correct as it stands |

**Common collocations for word-choice items**

| Noun / verb | Natural partner | Not used |
|---|---|---|
| heat | scorching, intense, sweltering | flaming, ardent |
| rain | heavy, torrential | strong, big |
| period | extended, long, prolonged | elongated, lengthened |
| aware | fully, well | outrightly |
| train / food | fast | speedy, quick (food: fast food) |
| decision | make / take | do |
| a speech | deliver, give, make | say, tell |
| a crime | commit | do |
| an exam | take / sit for / appear in | give (Indian usage, avoid in exams) |
| responsibility | take, accept, shoulder | make, give |
| a promise | make, keep, break | do |
| a mistake | make | do |
| homework / a favour / harm / one's duty | do | make |
| progress / an effort / a noise | make | do |

## 5. Fill in the blanks — confusable words

| Pair | Meaning | ✔ Example |

| affect / effect | verb: influence / noun: result | Smoking **affects** health. Its **effect** is harmful. |
| accept / except | receive / excluding | He **accepted** the gift. All **except** Ravi came. |
| elude / allude | escape / refer indirectly | The thief **eluded** the police. He **alluded** to his past. |
| eminent / imminent | famous / about to happen | an **eminent** scientist; an **imminent** storm |

## 6. Worked examples (how to think in the exam hall)

| Item | Walk-through | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| *The sparrows are / some of the few birds / that engage on / dust bathing.* ⭐ | Subject *sparrows* + *are* agree; *some of the few* is idiomatic; *engage* + activity needs **in**. | Segment 3: *engage in* |
| *It is so refreshing / to walk through / the beach / early in the morning.* ⭐ | Nothing wrong with the verb or tense. *Through* means inside something enclosed; a beach is an open surface. | *through the beach* → *along/on the beach* |
| *The Principal, along with / the teachers, / are planning / a farewell party.* | Subject = *Principal* (singular); *along with the teachers* is extra information. | *are planning* → *is planning* |
| *Rahul is one of the boys / who has / won medals / in the national games.* | *Who* refers to *boys* (plural), because many boys won and Rahul is one of them. | *who has* → *who have* |
| *She is suffering [with] high fever.* (improvement) | Fixed pair: *suffer from* an illness. Try each option in the sentence. | *from* |
| *I [am living] in Jaipur since my childhood.* (improvement) | *Since my childhood* + still living → present perfect continuous. | *have been living* |
| *The cost of living [have risen] sharply.* (improvement) | Head noun = *cost* (singular); *of living* does not change it. | *has risen* |
| *Neither the teacher [or the students] were aware.* (improvement) | *Neither* pairs with *nor*; the verb follows the nearer subject *students* → *were* is already right. | *nor the students* |
| *He is taller / than any other boy / in his class. / No error* | He is in the class, so *any other* is correct; comparative form is correct. | No error |
| *Post covid, international air travel is [hopping] back to normalcy.* ⭐ | Meaning test: the recovery was slow and painful, not lively. | *limping* |

**Timing**: an error-spotting item should take 30–40 seconds. If two segments still look possible after the 6-step check, mark the one with the verb or the preposition — those carry the error in most DSSSB items. Skip only if you cannot cut the options down to two (each wrong answer costs 0.25).

## Exam traps
1. **No sooner** takes **than**; **hardly, scarcely, barely** take **when** — and all take **had + subject + V3** ⭐.
2. *Had the company **launch*** ✘ — after *had* the verb is always **V3** (*launched*) ⭐.
3. **"No substitution required"** is a real option, not a trap answer — DSSSB used it for *gearing up for* ⭐ — but test every option before choosing it.
4. A segment can look fine alone; the error may be **agreement with a word in another segment** (*The Principal, along with the teachers, **are***).
5. **Being + adjective** (*her being honest*), not *being + noun* ⭐.
6. A **verb cannot modify a noun**: *detect dog* ✘ → *detection dog* ⭐.
7. **Though/although** never pairs with **but**, and **whether** never expresses contrast ⭐.
8. Word-choice items test **tone**: *hopping* (cheerful) vs *limping* (struggling) ⭐ — read the whole sentence for the mood.

## Quick self-check
1. Correct: *No sooner had the company launch its product than it went bankrupt.* — **had the company launched.** ⭐
2. Correct: *Barely has they won the match when…* — **Barely had they won.** ⭐
3. Better word for *hopping* in *air travel is hopping back to normalcy*? — **limping.** ⭐
4. Error segment: *I appreciate / her being honesty / about all / her mistakes.* — **her being honesty** (→ honest). ⭐
5. Error: *Despite of the rain the match was played.* — **despite of** → despite.
6. Error: *She gave me a very good advices.* — **advices** → advice.
7. Fill: *Scarcely had I reached the gate ___ the bell rang.* — **when.**
8. Correct: *If he will come tomorrow, I shall discuss it.* — **If he comes.**
9. Correct: *He returned back from Mumbai.* — **He returned from Mumbai.**
10. Fill: *The clever thief managed to ___ (elude/allude) the police.* — **elude.**
