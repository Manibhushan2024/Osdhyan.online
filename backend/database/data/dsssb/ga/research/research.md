# DSSSB Section A General Awareness: research notes (October 2026)

GA is 20 questions in TGT/PGT/PRT Section A (about 8 current affairs, 12 static). Non-teaching posts such as ASO give GA 40 marks and draw on the same pool.

**Evidence.** (1) Our 80 official TGT CS questions (4 papers, June 2023 and Feb 2024). (2) A reconstructed ASO 2026 GA replay (21–30 Sep 2026, about 40 GA questions per shift; unofficial). (3) Testbook DSSSB question banks for Patwari, LDC, MTS and general GA (undated). (4) Adda247 PGT 2025 summary (Rowlatt Act, Ugadi, NIDHI, RTI, CrPC). (5) A PagalGuy recall of DASS Grade 2 (2019). (6) PW TGT 2025 review: GA "moderate", 10–13 good attempts. No site has published a GA question list for TGT 2025 or Patwari (Oct 2026).

## (b) Current-affairs categories DSSSB favours

1. **Government schemes and MoUs** (most frequent): NAMASTE, Exim Bank–SIDBI fund, Mission Karmayogi, ministry MoUs (PYQs); Biopharma SHAKTI, Nasha Mukt Yuva (ASO 2026).
2. **Sports**: FIFA 2022, Ranji final, IBA boxing, TT and badminton medals, first woman IOA president (PYQs); tennis majors, Khelo India Winter Games, CWG host (ASO 2026).
3. **Awards**: Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize, Digital India Awards, Padma awards, Oscar and Pritzker (ASO 2026).
4. **Appointments**: Delhi LG in our PYQs; heads of international bodies in ASO 2026.
5. **Indices and reports**: SRS IMR in our PYQs; CCPI and SDG ranks, IIP base year and World Bank forecast in ASO.
6. **Science, tech and space**: ISRO MoUs, the Jio–IIT Bombay AI project, Dark Sky Reserve.
7. **Defence**: Agni-5 fuel type in our PYQs; Vayu Shakti, Suryastra and naval conferences in ASO.
8. **Delhi-located events**: Delhi LG; summits at Bharat Mandapam; Raisina Dialogue; Delhi Oxygen Parks (ASO 2026).
9. **Days and themes**: National Small Industries Day; Civil Services Day and Financial Literacy Week themes.
10. **Books, and bills or acts in news**: recent books by public figures; the Jan Vishwas and Supreme Court judges bills; PESA notification.

Window: events 6–12 months before the exam; typical stems are "which ministry/partner" and "which state first".

## (c) Parmar SSC's emphasis

Evidence: video titles, third-party summaries, notes and book listings only.
- **Scope:** SSC-first (also "like RRB and IB"); series Parmar GK 3.0/4.0, Free Revision Batch (FRB 2.0) and 2026–27 one-shots.
- **Static topics with seen evidence:** classical dances, folk dances and festivals of India (FRB and 2026–27 one-shots); titles such as "All folk dance questions asked by SSC in 2025"; a 132-question Static GK PYQ session; one-shots for history, biology, polity and economics. Notes and mind maps cover all GS subjects plus static GK. A "Books and Authors" file appears in a Scribd listing.
- **Style:** per a summary of the classical-dance video: mnemonics, numbered quiz MCQs, "frequently asked" flags, default-answer tips; drills states, gurus, gharanas, revivalists, Sattriya's 2000 recognition, Natya Shastra.
- **Current affairs:** a 224-page "Current Affair Shot" (2026) book and an Instagram "Daily Digest"; chapters not visible.
- **DSSSB or Delhi:** no Delhi- or DSSSB-specific Parmar content found. Use it for art and culture and SSC PYQ one-liners; take Delhi GK elsewhere.

- **Delhi GK** is thin (1 explicit question). Add monuments and builders, Art. 239AA/69th Amendment, Assembly (70) and Lok Sabha (7) seats, Yamuna, Ridge, Sultanate and Mughal Delhi, Delhi schemes and venues.
- **Ancient and medieval history:** Harappa, Vedic, Mauryas, Guptas, Vijayanagara and the Tripartite Struggle are missing
- **1857 and Gandhian movements:** nearly absent.
- **Polity:** schedules, emergency articles, DPSP and duties, the Seventh Schedule and landmark cases are missing.
- **Geography:** mountains, atmosphere layers, monsoon, waterways, national parks and Ramsar sites are missing.
- **Economy:** Five-Year Plans, 1991 reforms, NITI Aayog and methods of measuring national income are missing.
