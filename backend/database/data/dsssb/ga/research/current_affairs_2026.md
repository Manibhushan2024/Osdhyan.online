Prepared on 24 October 2026 for DSSSB (Delhi teacher recruitment) GA practice.

**How the items were checked**
- Every row cites the page on which the fact was confirmed.
- PRS Legislative Research monthly policy reviews (prsindia.org) were read in full.
- Other rows were confirmed from search-engine extracts of the cited page. Direct page fetch was blocked (HTTP 403 or timeout) for pib.gov.in and nobelprize.org.
- "Exam-prep source" means the fact was confirmed only on a coaching site (Drishti, Testbook, Adda247 and similar). Cross-check those rows before setting questions on them.
- **[ANNOUNCED - NOT YET HELD]** marks anything scheduled after 24 Oct 2026, or announced but not yet completed.
- Items from January 2026 appear only where the brief asked for them (Padma 2026) or where they explain a February to October event. Each is labelled "(context)".
