# Indian History, Art & Culture

**In 30 seconds**
- DSSSB asks **art & culture most** ⭐: dances, musicians + instruments, temples, caves, monuments, UNESCO sites, fairs.
- Ancient favourites: Indus sites, Buddhism/Jainism, Ashoka, Guptas, **Sangam epics** ⭐, **Palas** ⭐ (Dharmapala → Vikramashila).
- Medieval: **Delhi Sultanate** ⭐ dynasties, **Muhammad bin Tughluq's experiments** ⭐, **Mughal battles and buildings** ⭐.
- Modern: **Anglo–Mysore wars** ⭐ (Seringapatam 1799), **reform movements** ⭐ (Derozio – Young Bengal), **tribal/peasant revolts** ⭐ (leader–movement pairs).
- National movement: INC 1885 ⭐; know the 1905–1947 chain in order.
- Personalities ⭐: Naoroji first Asian British MP (1892), Gandhi's satyagrahas, Bose, Bhagat Singh.
- Post-independence ⭐: Operation Polo (Hyderabad 1948), **Operation Vijay (Goa 1961)**, SRA 1956.
- UNESCO: India has **44** sites (2025 count); Charminar, Jantar Mantar Delhi and Bibi ka Maqbara are NOT on the list.

## 1. Ancient India

### Indus Valley Civilisation (c. 2500–1900 BCE, mature phase)
| Site | River / State | Remember for |
|---|---|---|
| Harappa | Ravi, Pakistan | First found (Daya Ram Sahni, 1921); granaries |
| Mohenjo-daro | Indus, Sindh | R.D. Banerji 1922; Great Bath, bronze dancing girl, Pashupati seal, priest-king |
| Lothal | Bhogava, Gujarat | Dockyard, bead factory (S.R. Rao) |
| Kalibangan | Ghaggar, Rajasthan | Ploughed field, fire altars |
| Dholavira | Kutch, Gujarat | Water reservoirs, signboard, 3-part city; UNESCO 2021 |
| Rakhigarhi | Haryana | Largest Harappan site in India |
| Chanhudaro | Sindh | Bead-making; no citadel |

Features: grid streets, burnt bricks, drains, cotton, undeciphered script; no iron, no temples.

### Vedic age
- **Rig Veda**: oldest; 10 mandalas, 1,028 hymns; Gayatri Mantra (Mandala III); Purusha Sukta (X) first mentions four varnas. Indra most-hymned god, then Agni.
- Sama (music), Yajur (rituals), Atharva (magic, medicine).
- Battle of Ten Kings – Sudas, on the Parushni (Ravi).
- Later Vedic: iron (*shyama ayas*), Upanishads; "Satyameva Jayate" from **Mundaka Upanishad**.

### Buddhism and Jainism
| Point | Buddhism | Jainism |
|---|---|---|
| Founder | Gautama Buddha, b. Lumbini (c. 563 BCE) | Mahavira (24th Tirthankara), b. Kundagrama near Vaishali; 1st = Rishabhadeva, 23rd = Parshvanath |
| Enlightenment | Bodh Gaya, peepal tree, Niranjana river | Kaivalya at Jrimbhikagrama, sal tree |
| First sermon / Death | Sarnath / Kushinagar | — / Pawapuri |
| Core | 4 Noble Truths, Eightfold Path | Triratna; 5 vows (Mahavira added brahmacharya) |
| Texts | Tripitaka (Pali): Vinaya, Sutta, Abhidhamma | Agamas (compiled at Vallabhi) |
| Sects | Hinayana / Mahayana (split at 4th council) | Shvetambara / Digambara |

**Buddhist councils**: Rajagriha (Ajatashatru) → Vaishali (Kalashoka) → Pataliputra (Ashoka, Moggaliputta Tissa) → Kashmir (Kanishka, Vasumitra).
**Buddhist architecture** ⭐: **Stupa** (relic mound), **Chaitya** (prayer hall), **Vihara** (monastery). "Monka" is not a structure.

### 16 Mahajanapadas (Anguttara Nikaya) – key capitals
Magadha – Rajagriha, later Pataliputra · Kosala – Shravasti · Vatsa – Kaushambi · Avanti – Ujjain · Vajji – Vaishali (republic) · Anga – Champa · Kashi – Varanasi · Gandhara – Taxila · Kuru – Indraprastha · **Ashmaka – Potali (only one south of the Vindhyas)**.
Magadha dynasties: Haryanka (Bimbisara, Ajatashatru; Udayin founded Pataliputra) → Shishunaga → Nanda.

### Mauryas (c. 321–185 BCE)
- **Chandragupta**: guided by Kautilya (*Arthashastra*); Megasthenes (*Indica*) came from Seleucus; died a Jain at Shravanabelagola.
- **Ashoka**: Kalinga war (c. 261 BCE) → Dhamma. **Major Rock Edict XIII** describes Kalinga. Edicts mostly in **Brahmi**; Kharosthi in the north-west; Greek and Aramaic at Kandahar. **Maski** and Gujarra minor edicts name "Ashoka"; others say "Devanampiya Piyadasi". Deciphered by **James Prinsep (1837)**. Sarnath Lion Capital = national emblem.
- Last ruler Brihadratha killed by Pushyamitra Shunga.

### Guptas (c. 320–550 CE) – "classical age"
- Chandragupta I – Gupta era 319–20 CE; married a Lichchhavi princess.
- **Samudragupta** – Allahabad Pillar inscription (Harisena); "Napoleon of India" (V.A. Smith); veena coins.
- **Chandragupta II Vikramaditya** – Fa-Hien visited; Kalidasa at court; Mehrauli Iron Pillar.
- Kumaragupta I – founded **Nalanda**; Skandagupta – repelled the Huns.
- Science: Aryabhata (*Aryabhatiya*), Varahamihira (*Brihat Samhita*).

### Sangam age ⭐ (c. 3rd century BCE – 3rd century CE)
| Kingdom | Emblem | Capital / Port |
|---|---|---|
| Chera | Bow | Vanji / Muziris |
| Chola | Tiger | Uraiyur / Puhar (Karikala built Kallanai) |
| Pandya | Fish | Madurai / Korkai (patrons of the Sangams) |

| Work | Author |
|---|---|
| Tolkappiyam (grammar) | Tolkappiyar |
| Tirukkural ("Tamil Veda") | Thiruvalluvar |

Anthologies: Ettuthogai (8) + Pattupattu (10). The five great epics also include Valayapathi and Kundalakesi.

### Post-Gupta kingdoms – one line each
- **Harsha** (606–647): capitals Thanesar → Kannauj; Hiuen Tsang visited; Banabhatta wrote *Harshacharita*; defeated by **Pulakeshin II** on the Narmada (Aihole inscription, Ravikirti).
- **Palas ⭐** (Bengal, from c. 750): **Gopala** elected founder; **Dharmapala founded Vikramashila and Somapura** universities; Devapala; Tripartite struggle for Kannauj (with Pratiharas, Rashtrakutas).
- **Cholas**: Vijayalaya (Thanjavur); **Rajaraja I** – Brihadeeswara temple; **Rajendra I** – Gangaikonda Cholapuram, naval raid on Srivijaya; Uttaramerur inscriptions – village assemblies.
- **Rashtrakutas**: Dantidurga founder; Krishna I – Kailasa temple, Ellora; Amoghavarsha I – *Kavirajamarga*; capital Manyakheta.
- **Chalukyas of Badami**: Pulakeshin II; temples at Aihole, Badami, Pattadakal.

## 2. Medieval India

### Delhi Sultanate ⭐ (1206–1526)
| Dynasty | Founder | Key facts |
|---|---|---|
| Mamluk / Slave (1206–90) | Qutb-ud-din Aibak | Aibak began Qutb Minar. **Iltutmish** – silver *tanka*, copper *jital*, iqta, the Forty. **Razia** – first woman ruler. **Balban** – *sijda, paibos*, "blood and iron" |
| Khalji (1290–1320) | Jalal-ud-din Khalji | **Alauddin** – market control, *dagh* (branding) and *chehra*, Siri fort, Alai Darwaza, unfinished Alai Minar; Malik Kafur's southern campaigns |

| Lodi (1451–1526) | Bahlul Lodi | **Sikandar Lodi founded Agra (1504)**; Ibrahim Lodi lost First Panipat |

**Tughluq experiments ⭐ (Muhammad bin Tughluq, 1325–51)**
1. Capital shifted Delhi → **Daulatabad (Devagiri)**, c. 1327.
2. **Token currency** of copper/brass, c. 1329–30 – forged widely, withdrawn.
3. Higher tax in the Doab during famine.
4. **Khurasan expedition** ⭐ – huge army raised, paid for a year, disbanded unused.
5. **Qarachil** (Kumaon hills) expedition – disaster.
6. *Diwan-i-Kohi* – agriculture department. Ibn Battuta served as qazi of Delhi (wrote *Rihla*).

**Firoz Shah Tughluq**: canals, towns (Hisar, Firozabad, Jaunpur), moved two Ashokan pillars to Delhi, *Diwan-i-Khairat* (charity), *Diwan-i-Bandagan* (slaves), jizya on Brahmins.

## Exam traps
1. **Bhajan Sopori played santoor**, not veena.
2. **Silappadikaram – Ilango Adigal**; Kovalan is its hero; Sattanar wrote Manimekalai.
3. **Bibi ka Maqbara is in Aurangabad**, not Delhi; Alai Minar and Balban's tomb are in Delhi.
4. **Charminar is not a UNESCO site**; Delhi has only three (Red Fort, Qutb Minar, Humayun's Tomb). The UNESCO Jantar Mantar is in Jaipur.
5. **Operation Vijay** = Goa 1961 (and Kargil 1999); **Operation Polo** = Hyderabad 1948.
6. **Pabna – Ishan Chandra Roy/Shambhu Pal; Kharwar – Bhagirath Manjhi; Koya – Tomma Dora.** Papers swap these.
7. **Battle** of Seringapatam **1799** = 4th Anglo–Mysore war; **Treaty** of Seringapatam **1792** = 3rd war.
8. **Dharmapala** (not Gopala) founded Vikramashila and Somapura; Nalanda is Gupta (Kumaragupta I).
