# Indian Geography & Census

**In 30 seconds**
- India lies between 8°4′N–37°6′N and 68°7′E–97°25′E; area 32.87 lakh sq km (7th largest, 2.4% of world land). Standard Meridian 82°30′E passes through Mirzapur (UP).
- DSSSB 2023–24 asked: Purvanchal hills, the Mesozoic era, the source of the Narmada, Bangar/Khadar, lignite in Tamil Nadu, mines by state, and four Census 2011 facts.
- Himalaya = Himadri + Himachal + Shiwalik. Purvanchal = Patkai, Naga, Manipur and Mizo hills. The Shevaroy Hills are in the Eastern Ghats.
- Mesozoic = age of dinosaurs. Gondwana rocks hold about 98% of India's coal. Deccan Trap basalt weathers into black soil.
- Godavari is the longest peninsular river. Narmada and Tapi flow west through rift valleys into the Arabian Sea.
- Alluvial soil (Khadar = new, Bangar = old) covers the most area. Black soil is the cotton soil.
- Census 2011: 121.09 crore people, decadal growth 17.7%, density 382, sex ratio 943, literacy 74.04%, rural 68.84%.
- Census 2027 will be India's first digital census and will count caste. Reference date: 1 March 2027.

## 3. Geological time scale ⭐

| Era (million years ago) | Periods | Life | Indian example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Precambrian (4,600–541) | Hadean, Archean, Proterozoic eons | Bacteria, algae | Archaean gneiss; **Dharwar** (oldest metamorphosed sediments; iron ore, Kolar/Hutti gold); Cuddapah; Vindhyan (sandstone of Red Fort, Panna diamonds) |
| Palaeozoic (541–252) | Cambrian, Ordovician, Silurian, Devonian, Carboniferous, Permian | Fish (Devonian = age of fishes), amphibians, coal forests | **Lower Gondwana coal** (Carboniferous–Permian): Damodar valley |

| Cenozoic (66–now) | Palaeogene, Neogene, Quaternary (Pleistocene, Holocene) | Age of mammals; humans in Quaternary | Himalayan uplift; Tertiary oil (Assam, Gujarat) and lignite (Neyveli); Gangetic alluvium |

## 6. Climate and monsoon

- India has a tropical monsoon climate. The south-west monsoon reaches Kerala around **1 June** and covers the whole country by about 8 July.
- Two branches: **Arabian Sea branch** (Western Ghats, west coast) and **Bay of Bengal branch** (North-East, Ganga plain). Mawsynram and Cherrapunji (Meghalaya) get the most rain; Jaisalmer and Leh the least.
- **Retreating (north-east) monsoon**, October–December, gives most of Tamil Nadu's rain. "October heat" follows the withdrawal.
- **Western disturbances** come from the Mediterranean and give winter rain and snow to the north-west; this helps the rabi wheat crop.
- Local storms: mango showers (Kerala, Karnataka), Kalbaisakhi or Nor'westers (Bengal, Assam), loo (hot dry wind, northern plains). El Niño weakens the monsoon; La Niña strengthens it.

## 10. Industries and transport

- **Iron and steel:** TISCO Jamshedpur (1907), Bhilai (USSR), Rourkela (Germany), Durgapur (UK), Bokaro (USSR), Visakhapatnam (shore-based).
- **Textiles:** first cotton mill Fort Gloster, Kolkata (1818); first successful mill Mumbai (1854); Mumbai is the "Manchester of India". Jute mills line the Hugli (first, Rishra 1855).
- **Industrial regions:** Mumbai–Pune, Hugli, Bengaluru–Tamil Nadu, Gujarat (Ahmedabad–Vadodara), Chotanagpur, Visakhapatnam–Guntur, Gurugram–Delhi–Meerut, Kollam–Thiruvananthapuram.
- **Roads:** the longest NH is NH 44 (Srinagar–Kanyakumari, about 3,745 km). The Golden Quadrilateral links Delhi–Mumbai–Chennai–Kolkata.
- **Railways:** first train 16 April 1853, Bori Bunder–Thane. 17 zones (South Coast Railway, Visakhapatnam, being set up as the 18th). Chenab Bridge (J&K) is the world's highest railway arch bridge (opened 2025). Longest platform: Hubballi.
- **Ports:** 12 major ports (Vadhavan, Maharashtra, under construction). JNPT is the largest container port; Mumbai the largest natural harbour; Deendayal (Kandla) a tidal port in Gujarat; Visakhapatnam the deepest landlocked harbour; Kolkata a riverine port; Vizhinjam (Kerala) the first deep-water transshipment port (2025).
- **Waterways and airports:** NW-1 (Ganga, Prayagraj–Haldia) is the longest waterway. IGI Delhi is the busiest airport; Kochi was the world's first fully solar-powered airport.

## Exam traps

1. Highest peak "in India" is Kangchenjunga; highest wholly inside India is Nanda Devi; K2 is in PoK.
2. The Shevaroy Hills are in the Eastern Ghats (TN), not Purvanchal.
3. Mesozoic = dinosaurs; Cenozoic = mammals; the Palaeozoic came before dinosaurs.
4. Narmada rises at Amarkantak; Godavari at Trimbakeshwar; Krishna at Mahabaleshwar; Kaveri in the Brahmagiri hills.
5. Khadar is the NEW alluvium; Bangar is the OLD one with kankar.
6. The largest lignite reserves are in Tamil Nadu, not Gujarat. Most mines by number: MP; highest mineral value: Odisha.
7. The Tropic of Cancer does NOT cross Odisha or Bihar; the Standard Meridian DOES cross Odisha.
8. Census 2011: highest SC sex ratio is Kerala, then Puducherry (not Tamil Nadu); only Nagaland had negative growth.
