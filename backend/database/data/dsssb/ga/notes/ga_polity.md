# Indian Polity & Constitution

**In 30 seconds**
- Constitution adopted 26 November 1949, in force 26 January 1950; today about 470 articles, 25 parts, 12 schedules.
- Fundamental Rights = Part III (Art 12–35) ⭐; Art 21 = life and personal liberty ⭐; Art 32 = "heart and soul" (Ambedkar).
- Vice-President is ex-officio Chairman of Rajya Sabha (Art 64) ⭐ and acts as President in a vacancy (Art 65) ⭐.
- Amendment = Art 368 ⭐; 7th Amendment (1956) allowed one Governor for two or more states ⭐.
- Panchayats = Part IX, 73rd Amendment; minimum age for a panchayat member is 21 (Art 243F) ⭐.
- Finance Commission = Art 280; statutory grants-in-aid = Art 275 ⭐; discretionary grants = Art 282.
- Official language: Art 343 — Hindi in Devanagari script ⭐; Part XVII (Art 343–351).
- UP has the largest Assembly (403 seats) ⭐; UPSC and State PSCs = Art 315 ⭐; ECI = Art 324.

**Preamble**: "We, the people of India" — India is a Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic Republic; it secures Justice (social, economic, political), Liberty (thought, expression, belief, faith, worship), Equality (status, opportunity) and Fraternity (dignity of the individual, unity and integrity of the Nation).
- Amended only once: **42nd Amendment (1976)** added **Socialist, Secular, Integrity**.
- Berubari case (1960): not a part of the Constitution; Kesavananda Bharati (1973): it **is** a part; LIC case (1995) repeated this.

## 5. Amendment procedure ⭐ (Art 368, borrowed from South Africa)

| Type | Majority | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Simple majority (outside Art 368) | Ordinary | New states (Art 2, 3), Legislative Councils (169), citizenship, quorum |
| Special majority | Majority of total membership + 2/3 of present and voting, in **each** House | Fundamental Rights, DPSP |
| Special majority + half the states | As above + ratified by at least half the state legislatures | Election of President, SC/HC, Seventh Schedule, states in Parliament, Art 368 itself, GST |

- Bill can start in either House; no prior permission of President; no joint sitting; President **must** give assent (24th Amendment).

## 6. Parliament ⭐

| Item | Lok Sabha | Rajya Sabha |
|---|---|---|
| Maximum strength | 550 (530 states + 20 UTs) | 250 (238 elected + 12 nominated) |
| Present | 543 elected | 245 (233 + 12) |
| Election | Direct | By elected MLAs, single transferable vote |
| Term | 5 years (can be dissolved) | Permanent; one-third retire every 2 years; member's term 6 years |
| Minimum age | 25 | 30 |
| Presiding officer | Speaker (elected by LS) | Vice-President, ex-officio Chairman ⭐ |
| Special powers | Money Bill, no-confidence motion | Art 249 (State List), Art 312 (new All India Service) |

- **Sessions**: Budget, Monsoon, Winter; gap ≤ 6 months (Art 85). President summons and prorogues; only Lok Sabha is dissolved. Quorum = one-tenth. Question Hour = first hour; Zero Hour = Indian innovation (1962).
- **Bills**: ordinary (either House); Money Bill (Art 110: only in LS, President's recommendation, Speaker certifies, RS must return within **14 days**, its recommendations not binding); financial bills (Art 117); amendment bills (Art 368).
- **Joint sitting** (Art 108): called by President, presided over by Speaker; not for Money Bills or amendment bills. Held 3 times — Dowry Prohibition (1961), Banking Service Commission Repeal (1978), POTA (2002).
- **Speaker**: removed by LS resolution with 14 days' notice; casting vote only; decides Money Bills and defection cases (subject to judicial review — Kihoto Hollohan, 1992). Pro tem Speaker swears in new members.
- **Financial committees**: PAC (22: 15 LS + 7 RS; chair from opposition by convention); Estimates Committee (30, all LS); Committee on Public Undertakings (22). 24 Departmentally Related Standing Committees (since 1993).
- UP sends the most MPs: 80 (LS), 31 (RS). Delhi: 7 LS, 3 RS.

## 7. State legislature ⭐

- Governor + Assembly (+ Council where it exists). Assembly: max 500, min 60 (exceptions: Sikkim 32, Goa and Mizoram 40). MLA age 25, MLC age 30.
- Largest Assemblies: **UP 403** ⭐, West Bengal 294, Maharashtra 288, Bihar 243, Tamil Nadu 234. Delhi has 70.
- **Bicameral states (6)**: Andhra Pradesh, Bihar, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Telangana, Uttar Pradesh. J&K's Council ended in 2019.
- **Art 169**: Parliament creates/abolishes a Council by law if the Assembly passes a resolution by special majority; not treated as an Art 368 amendment.
- Council size: not more than one-third of the Assembly, minimum 40. Members: 1/3 by MLAs, 1/3 by local bodies, 1/12 graduates, 1/12 teachers, 1/6 nominated by Governor.
- Council can delay an ordinary bill at most 4 months (3 + 1) and a Money Bill 14 days.

## 9. Centre–state relations ⭐

| List (7th Schedule) | Items (now / originally) | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Union | 100 / 97 | Defence, foreign affairs, banking, railways, atomic energy |
| State | 61 / 66 | Police, public order, public health, agriculture, land |
| Concurrent | 52 / 47 | Education, forests, marriage, criminal law, electricity |

Residuary power: Parliament (Art 248). Union law prevails in a Concurrent conflict (Art 254).

**Financial relations ⭐**
- **Finance Commission (Art 280)**: quasi-judicial; President appoints chairman + 4 members every 5 years; recommends sharing of taxes (vertical and horizontal) and grants under Art 275. 1st FC: K.C. Neogy (1951). 15th: N.K. Singh (41% share to states, 2021–26). 16th: Arvind Panagariya (award 2026–31; its share and accepted figures — check the current-affairs capsule).
- **GST Council (Art 279A)**: Union Finance Minister (chair) + state Finance Ministers; decisions need 3/4 weighted vote (Centre 1/3, states 2/3).
- **Grants**: Art 275 statutory grants-in-aid, charged on Consolidated Fund, on FC advice ⭐; Art 282 discretionary grants for any public purpose.
- Taxes: Art 265 — no tax except by authority of law; Art 270 — central taxes shared with states.

## 11. Judiciary

- **Supreme Court**: 34 judges including CJI (2019 Act); retire at 65; seat Delhi (Art 130). Qualification: 5 years HC judge, or 10 years HC advocate, or distinguished jurist.
- **High Courts**: 25; judges retire at 62; Art 226 writs are wider than Art 32 (also for legal rights). Oldest: Calcutta, Bombay, Madras (1862). Delhi HC 1966.
- **Removal** of SC/HC judge: proved misbehaviour or incapacity; special majority in each House (Judges Inquiry Act 1968).
- **Writs**: Habeas Corpus (produce the body), Mandamus (we command), Prohibition (stop a lower court), Certiorari (quash an order), Quo Warranto (by what authority).
- **PIL**: Justices P.N. Bhagwati and V.R. Krishna Iyer (1980s); relaxed locus standi.
- **Judicial review**: from USA; Art 13, 32, 226; part of basic structure.
- **Collegium**: CJI + 4 senior-most judges recommend SC appointments (Second Judges case 1993, Third 1998); 99th Amendment's NJAC struck down 2015 (Fourth Judges case).

## 13. Citizenship and elections

- **Citizenship**: Part II (Art 5–11); Citizenship Act 1955. Acquired by birth, descent, registration, naturalisation, incorporation of territory. Lost by renunciation, termination, deprivation. Single citizenship (UK). **CAA 2019** (rules March 2024): Hindu, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, Parsi, Christian migrants from Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Pakistan who entered by 31 December 2014.
- **ECI**: founded 25 January 1950 (National Voters' Day); first CEC Sukumar Sen; three-member since 1993. 2023 Act: selection panel = PM, Leader of Opposition in LS, a Union Cabinet minister named by PM.
- **EVM**: first used 1982, Paravur (Kerala). **VVPAT**: first 2013, Noksen (Nagaland). NOTA: 2013 (SC order).
- **Delimitation**: Commissions 1952, 1963, 1973, 2002; seat numbers frozen till the first census after 2026 (84th Amendment).
- First general election: 1951–52.

## Exam traps
1. Rajya Sabha Chairman is the **Vice-President**, not the President or Speaker ⭐.
2. Art 65 = VP acting as President ⭐; Art 63 only creates the office; Art 61 is impeachment.
3. One Governor for 2+ states came by the **7th** Amendment, not the 42nd ⭐.
4. Panchayat member age is **21** (not 18 or 25) ⭐.
5. Art 275 = statutory grants on FC advice; Art 282 = discretionary ⭐.
6. Art 343 = Union's official language; the Constitution names no "national language" ⭐.
7. Joint sitting is chaired by the Speaker and never used for Money or amendment bills.
8. NITI Aayog is neither constitutional nor statutory; Finance Commission and ECI are constitutional.
