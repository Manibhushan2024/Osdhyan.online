# General Awareness — Fast Revision Sheet

## How to use this sheet
- Section A has **20 GA questions** at 1 mark each: about **8 current affairs + 12 static GK**. A wrong answer costs **−0.25**, so skip a pure guess but attempt anything you can narrow to two options.
- ⭐ = asked in the four DSSSB TGT CS papers of 2023–24 (80 real GA questions); in current affairs it marks the same kind of item (Delhi LG, repo rate, FIFA Golden Boot, Ranji final, Agni, BWF Worlds). Capsule labels are kept: "(context)", **[ANNOUNCED - NOT YET HELD]**, "(exam-prep source)" = confirmed only on a coaching site.
- Read order (60–90 min): Top 30 → Current Affairs top 40 → History, Art & Culture (most-asked static) → Polity → Geography & Census → Science → Economy → Delhi GK → memory hooks → last check.

## Indian Economy & Banking Basics

### National income ⭐
| Term | Formula / meaning |
|---|---|
| GDP | Final goods and services produced **within** the country in a year |
| GNP | GDP + net factor income from abroad (NFIA) |
| NDP | GDP − Depreciation |
| **NNP** ⭐ | **GNP − Depreciation** |
| National Income | NNP at factor cost; market price = factor cost + net indirect taxes |
| GDP deflator | (Nominal GDP ÷ Real GDP) × 100 |
| Measured by | NSO (MoSPI); first estimate by Dadabhai Naoroji |
| Indices | CPI by NSO (RBI target 4% ± 2%); WPI by the Office of the Economic Adviser, DPIIT |
| Largest state economy ⭐ | **Maharashtra**, then Tamil Nadu (RBI Handbook) |

### Budget basics ⭐
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Budget | Art 112, "Annual Financial Statement"; presented 1 Feb since 2017; Railway Budget merged 2017 |
| **Revenue receipts** ⭐ | Tax and non-tax; create **no liability**, reduce **no asset** |
| Capital receipts | Borrowings, loan recovery, disinvestment |
| Revenue deficit | Revenue expenditure − revenue receipts |
| Fiscal deficit | Total expenditure − (revenue receipts + non-debt capital receipts) = total borrowing |
| Primary deficit | Fiscal deficit − interest payments |
| FRBM Act / Economic Survey | 2003 / Chief Economic Adviser, a day before the Budget |

### RBI rates and instruments ⭐
| Tool | Meaning (October 2026 value) |
|---|---|
| **Repo** ⭐ | Banks borrow from RBI by selling securities with a buy-back promise (**5.50%**) |

| SDF | Banks park surplus without collateral; floor = repo − 0.25% (**5.25%**) |
| MSF / Bank rate | Emergency borrowing; ceiling = repo + 0.25% (**5.75%**) |
| CRR / SLR | Cash kept with RBI, no interest / kept by banks in cash, gold, securities |
| MPC | 6 members (3 RBI + 3 external); Governor has a casting vote |
| RBI | Started 1 April 1935; nationalised 1 January 1949; ₹1 note issued by GoI |
