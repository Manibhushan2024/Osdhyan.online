# Delhi GK

**In 30 seconds**
- Delhi = National Capital Territory (NCT), a Union Territory **with a legislature** under **Art 239AA ⭐** (added by the **69th Amendment, 1991**).
- Area **1,483 km²**; Haryana on three sides, Uttar Pradesh on the east; river **Yamuna**; northern tip of the **Aravalli** (the Ridge).
- **70** Assembly seats, **7** Lok Sabha seats, **3** Rajya Sabha seats. CM **Rekha Gupta** (since 20 Feb 2025); LG **Taranjit Singh Sandhu** (since 11 Mar 2026).[^cm][^lg]
- Districts: **11 → 13** revenue districts from **1 Jan 2026**.[^dist] MCD reunified **2022**, **250 wards**.
- The "seven cities" run from Lal Kot (Tomars) to Shahjahanabad (Shah Jahan); **New Delhi** is the eighth: capital shift announced **12 Dec 1911**, inaugurated **13 Feb 1931**.
- UNESCO sites: **Qutub Minar** and **Humayun's Tomb** (1993), **Red Fort Complex** (2007) ⭐.
- **Census 2011**: 1.68 crore people, density **11,320/km²** (highest in India), sex ratio **868**, literacy **86.2%**.
- **DSSSB** set up **4 Oct 1996**; **DBSE** (Delhi Board of School Education) **2021**; Happiness Curriculum **2018**.

## 2. Geography
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Area | **1,483 km²** |
| Neighbours | Only two: **Haryana** (N, W, S) and **Uttar Pradesh** (E) |
| River | **Yamuna** enters at **Palla**, leaves near **Jaitpur/Okhla**; barrages at Wazirabad, ITO, Okhla |
| Ridge | Northern tip of the **Aravalli**: Northern, Central, South-Central (Mehrauli) and Southern Ridge; **Asola Bhatti Wildlife Sanctuary** is on the Southern Ridge |
| Drainage | Najafgarh drain (old **Sahibi** river) is the largest drain into the Yamuna |
| Climate | Semi-arid, extreme: hot summers, cold winters, monsoon Jul–Sep |
| Districts | **11** (2012–2025) → **13** revenue districts and **39** sub-divisions from 1 Jan 2026, made co-terminous with MCD zones, NDMC and the Cantonment[^dist] |
| NCR | Delhi + parts of Haryana, UP and Rajasthan; **NCR Planning Board** under the **NCRPB Act, 1985** (Union Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs) |

## 3. Administration and polity

| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Status | 1912 Chief Commissioner's province → 1952 **Part C State** with an assembly → 1956 **UT** (assembly abolished) → 1966 Metropolitan Council → **1991** NCT with assembly |
| **Art 239AA ⭐** | Assembly can make laws on State and Concurrent List subjects **except public order, police and land** |
| Art 239AB | President can suspend Art 239AA if the constitutional machinery fails |
| **69th Amendment, 1991** | Inserted Arts 239AA and 239AB (based on the **Balakrishnan Committee**, 1989) |
| **GNCTD Act, 1991** | Law for the assembly and ministry; first election under it **1993** |
| GNCTD (Amendment) Act, 2021 | "Government" in Delhi laws means the **LG** |
| GNCTD (Amendment) Act, **2023** | Followed the May 2023 SC verdict on services; created the **National Capital Civil Service Authority** (CM chairs) for officers' transfers and postings |
| LG | Appointed by the **President**; first LG **Aditya Nath Jha** (1966) |
| CM & ministers | CM appointed by the **President**; ministers ≤ **10%** of assembly strength (CM + 6) |
| Assembly | **70** seats (12 SC), unicameral, 5-year term |
| MCD | Est. **1958**; split into 3 in **2012**; reunified **22 May 2022**; **250 wards**, **12 zones** |
| NDMC | New Delhi Municipal Council (**NDMC Act 1994**); chairperson appointed by the Centre |
| Delhi Cantonment Board | Under the **Ministry of Defence** |
| Delhi Police | Under the Union **Ministry of Home Affairs**; Commissioner system since **1978** |
| Delhi High Court | Est. **31 Oct 1966** |

## Exam traps
1. **Okhla Bird Sanctuary** is in **Noida (UP)**, not Delhi; Asola Bhatti is in Delhi.
2. **Surajkund Mela** is held in **Faridabad, Haryana**, not Delhi.
3. The Delhi assembly **cannot** make laws on **police, public order and land** (Art 239AA); it can on education and health.
4. Nehru hoisted the flag at the **Red Fort on 16 Aug 1947**, not 15 Aug.
5. The capital shift was **announced in 1911**, but New Delhi was **inaugurated in 1931**. Do not mix the years.
6. Delhi now has **13** revenue districts (from Jan 2026); older books say 11. The MCD has **12 zones and 250 wards**.
7. **First woman CM** = Sushma Swaraj; **first woman LoP** = Atishi; Rekha Gupta is the **4th** woman CM.
8. **Arun Jaitley Stadium** is the renamed stadium; the ground keeps the name **Feroz Shah Kotla**. Major Dhyan Chand Stadium (not JLN) hosted the **1951** Asian Games.

## Quick self-check
1. **Q:** Which article gives Delhi a legislative assembly? **A:** Art 239AA (69th Amendment, 1991).
2. **Q:** Name the three subjects outside the Delhi assembly's law-making power. **A:** Public order, police, land.
3. **Q:** Who built Firozabad and what remains of it? **A:** Firoz Shah Tughlaq; Feroz Shah Kotla.
4. **Q:** Which three Delhi monuments are UNESCO sites? **A:** Qutub Minar, Humayun's Tomb (1993), Red Fort Complex (2007).
5. **Q:** Who was Delhi's first Chief Minister? **A:** Chaudhary Brahm Prakash (1952).
6. **Q:** How many wards does the unified MCD have? **A:** 250.
7. **Q:** Which stadium hosted the first Asian Games (1951)? **A:** National Stadium (now Major Dhyan Chand National Stadium).
8. **Q:** Who is Delhi's Lieutenant Governor as of October 2026? **A:** Taranjit Singh Sandhu (since 11 Mar 2026).
9. **Q:** What does DSSSB recruit? **A:** Group B (non-gazetted) and Group C staff for the Delhi government and its local bodies.
10. **Q:** What was Delhi's Census 2011 sex ratio? **A:** 868 females per 1,000 males.
