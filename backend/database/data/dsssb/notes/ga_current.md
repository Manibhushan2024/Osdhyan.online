## 4. Government, economy and schemes

- **Union Budget 2026-27** (1 February 2026): Nirmala Sitharaman's ninth consecutive budget. Fiscal deficit target 4.3 per cent of GDP; record capital expenditure about Rs 12.2 lakh crore; no change in income tax slabs. Announcements included India Semiconductor Mission 2.0 and Biopharma SHAKTI.
- **Income Tax Act, 2025** replaced the 1961 Act with effect from 1 April 2026.
- **GST reform** (effective 22 September 2025): main slabs now 5 per cent and 18 per cent, with a special higher rate for sin and luxury goods.
- **Four Labour Codes** came into force on 21 November 2025, replacing 29 central labour laws.
- **VB-G RAM G Act, 2025**: replaced MGNREGA from 1 July 2026; guarantees 125 days of wage work per rural household; most states share 40 per cent of the cost (Centre 60 per cent).
- **RBI**: Governor Sanjay Malhotra. At its 5-7 October 2026 meeting the Monetary Policy Committee raised the repo rate by 25 basis points to 5.50 per cent, its first hike since 2023, and moved the stance from neutral to calibrated tightening. Next meeting 2-4 December 2026.
- **Census 2027**: India's first digital census. Phase 1 (houselisting) ran from April 2026; population enumeration has reference date 1 March 2027 (1 October 2026 for snow-bound areas). Caste will be counted for the first time in a census since 1931.
