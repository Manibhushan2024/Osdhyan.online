# General Awareness (Static GK): DSSSB TGT Revision Notes

## 1. One-page summary

- About 20 questions in the paper (20 marks). Typical split: 4-5 polity, 3-4 history, 3 geography, 2 economy, 2-3 science, 2 Delhi GK, 3-4 current affairs and days.
- Wrong answers cost 0.25 mark. Static GK is cheap marks: learn the tables below once, then revise only the one-liners in section 10.
- Delhi GK and teacher-related days (Teachers' Day, Education Day, Literacy Day) are favourites of DSSSB, so do not skip them.

**Parliament and judiciary numbers**

- Lok Sabha: maximum 550; 543 elected at present; term 5 years. Rajya Sabha: maximum 250 (238 elected + 12 nominated); permanent House, one-third retire every 2 years.
- Minimum age: President 35, Vice-President 35, Lok Sabha 25, Rajya Sabha 30.
- Money Bill: only in Lok Sabha; Rajya Sabha returns it in 14 days. Joint sitting is presided over by the Speaker (not for Money Bills or constitutional amendments).
- Supreme Court: 34 judges including the CJI; retirement 65 years (High Court 62). Basic structure doctrine: Kesavananda Bharati (1973).
- Five writs: Habeas Corpus (produce the body), Mandamus (we command), Prohibition, Certiorari, Quo Warranto (by what authority).
- Six Fundamental Rights (Art 14-32): equality, freedom, against exploitation, religion, cultural and educational, constitutional remedies. DPSPs are in Part IV (Art 36-51), non-justiciable, borrowed from Ireland.
- Constitution: Assembly met 9 Dec 1946, adopted 26 Nov 1949, enforced 26 Jan 1950. Drafting Committee chair Dr Ambedkar; President of Assembly Dr Rajendra Prasad.

## 4. Indian geography lists

- **Himalayan rivers** (perennial): Indus, Ganga, Brahmaputra. **Peninsular**: Godavari (longest, 1,465 km), Krishna, Kaveri, Mahanadi (east); Narmada and Tapi (west, rift valley).
- **Yamuna** rises at Yamunotri; **Ganga** at Gangotri (Bhagirathi) and becomes Ganga at Devprayag.
- **Soils**: alluvial (Indo-Gangetic plain, rabi crops), black or regur (basalt, cotton), red (iron oxide), laterite (heavy rain, cashew), desert, mountain.
- **Peaks**: Kangchenjunga (highest in India), Nanda Devi, Anamudi (highest in peninsular India, 2,695 m).
- **Tropic of Cancer** crosses 8 states: Gujarat, Rajasthan, MP, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura, Mizoram. Standard meridian 82°30' E (Mirzapur).
- **Seasons**: monsoon onset at Kerala around 1 June; western disturbances give winter rain in the north-west.
- **Lakes**: Chilika (largest coastal lagoon, Odisha), Wular (largest freshwater, J&K), Sambhar (largest inland salt lake, Rajasthan), Loktak (Manipur).
- **States**: 28 states and 8 Union Territories. Largest by area Rajasthan, smallest Goa; most populous Uttar Pradesh; longest coastline Gujarat.
- **Channels and straits**: Palk Strait (India-Sri Lanka), Ten Degree Channel (Andaman-Nicobar), Eight Degree (Minicoy-Maldives).
- **Minerals**: coking coal Jharia (Jharkhand); iron ore Odisha and Jharkhand; mica Jharkhand; copper Khetri (Rajasthan); bauxite Odisha.
- **Dams**: Bhakra (Sutlej), Hirakud (Mahanadi, longest), Tehri (highest), Sardar Sarovar (Narmada).
- **Mangroves**: Sundarbans (Ganga-Brahmaputra delta, West Bengal).

## 5. Economy terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Repo rate | Rate at which RBI lends to banks; reverse repo is the opposite |
| CRR / SLR | Cash held with RBI / liquid assets held by banks |
| Fiscal deficit | Total expenditure minus receipts excluding borrowings |
| Revenue deficit | Revenue expenditure minus revenue receipts |
| Primary deficit | Fiscal deficit minus interest payments |
| Direct tax | Income tax, corporation tax; indirect: GST, customs |
| GST Council | Art 279A; chaired by Union Finance Minister |
| CPI | RBI's inflation measure; target 4 per cent (2-6 band) |
| GDP | Value of goods and services produced inside the country in a year |
| Primary / secondary / tertiary | Agriculture and mining / industry / services |

- RBI: set up 1 April 1935, nationalised 1949, headquarters Mumbai. SEBI regulates securities; NABARD agriculture credit (1982).
- Budget: Article 112 (Annual Financial Statement), presented on 1 February. Funds: Consolidated Fund of India (Art 266), Contingency Fund (Art 267).
- Plans: First (1951, Harrod-Domar, agriculture), Second (Mahalanobis, heavy industry), Twelfth was the last (2012-17). NITI Aayog replaced the Planning Commission on 1 January 2015.

## 6. Class 10 science quick facts

- Baking soda NaHCO3; washing soda Na2CO3.10H2O; bleaching powder CaOCl2; Plaster of Paris CaSO4.1/2 H2O (from gypsum at 373 K).
- Reactivity series: K, Na, Ca, Mg, Al, Zn, Fe, Pb, (H), Cu, Hg, Ag, Au. Na and K are kept in kerosene.
- Rusting needs air and moisture. Graphite conducts, diamond is hardest. Alkane CnH2n+2, alkene CnH2n, alkyne CnH2n-2. Vinegar is ethanoic acid.
- Life processes: salivary amylase digests starch; small intestine absorbs food; chloroplast does photosynthesis. Mendel's dihybrid ratio 9:3:3:1; sex determined by the father's chromosome.
- Optics: myopia (concave lens), hypermetropia (convex). Power P = 1/f (metres), unit dioptre. Plane mirror image is virtual and same size.
- Electricity: V = IR; parallel 1/R = 1/R1 + 1/R2; power P = VI; fuse has low melting point. Generator works on electromagnetic induction; motor on force on a current-carrying conductor (Fleming's left-hand rule).
- Environment: 10 per cent energy transfer between trophic levels; ozone layer absorbs UV, depleted by CFCs.

## 7. Delhi GK

**Cities of Delhi**: Lal Kot (Tomars), Siri (Alauddin Khalji), Tughlaqabad, Jahanpanah, Firozabad (Firoz Shah Tughlaq), Dinpanah (Humayun), Shahjahanabad (Shah Jahan), New Delhi (1911-31).

| Monument | Fact |
|---|---|
| Red Fort | Shah Jahan, 1639-48; UNESCO site 2007 |
| Qutb Minar | Begun by Aibak, completed by Iltutmish; 72.5 m; UNESCO 1993 |
| Humayun's Tomb | Built from 1565 by Hamida Banu Begum; UNESCO 1993 |
| Jama Masjid | Shah Jahan, 1656 |
| Jantar Mantar | Sawai Jai Singh II, 1724 (not a UNESCO site in Delhi) |
| India Gate | Lutyens; WWI and Afghan War memorial |
| Rashtrapati Bhavan | Lutyens; formerly Viceroy's House |
| Iron Pillar | Mehrauli; Gupta period |

- Delhi has 3 UNESCO World Heritage Sites: Red Fort, Qutb Minar, Humayun's Tomb.
- Government: NCT of Delhi (69th Amendment, 1991; Art 239AA); Assembly of 70 seats; 7 Lok Sabha and 3 Rajya Sabha seats; head of state is the Lieutenant Governor. First CM Chaudhary Brahm Prakash (1952); longest-serving Sheila Dikshit (1998-2013); Rekha Gupta is CM since February 2025.
- MCD: unified in May 2022, 250 wards. Delhi High Court: 1966. DSSSB: 1996. Delhi Metro: first line 24 December 2002, DMRC founded 1995 (E. Sreedharan).
- Education: Directorate of Education runs government schools; Delhi School Education Act and Rules, 1973 govern recognised schools.
- Delhi facts: area 1,483 sq km, Yamuna on the east, Ridge in the south.
