## Notes format (SHORT)
Each chapter: `notes/<key>.md`, 2,500–3,500 words (cf_se about 2,000), almost all tables and one-line bullets. Start with "**In 30 seconds**" (6–8 bullets); mark concepts asked in the papers "⭐ Asked in DSSSB"; end with "## Exam traps" (8–10), "## One-liners for revision" (30–40) and "## Quick self-check" (10 questions with answers). Standard references: Morris Mano (digital), Stallings/Hamacher (architecture), Pressman/Sommerville (SE), MDN / W3C (web), Laudon (MIS), NCERT/B.Ed. texts and NEP 2020 (teaching), official Indian acts for law. Verify every numerical (conversions, K-maps, counters, complement arithmetic, Excel formulas) with a script.
