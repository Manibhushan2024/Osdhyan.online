## Rules that win marks (the simulator trains you for these)

1. **Never leave a question "Answered & Marked for Review".** DSSSB does not evaluate it — it scores zero. Before
   pressing *Submit* on a group, open every purple question and press *Save & Next*.
2. A selected option is **not saved** until *Save & Next* or *Mark for Review & Next*.
3. **Groups lock.** Once you submit a group you cannot go back. Budget **~12 minutes per group** of 20.
4. **Guess only after eliminating two options.** At −0.25, a 50/50 guess still gains; a blind guess loses.
5. After every mock, open the **result**: rank, percentile, section bars against average and topper, the Venn of
   attempted vs marked, and *What to improve*. Then open **Solutions**, use **Revise this concept** on every wrong
   answer, and retake that chapter's test within 48 hours.
